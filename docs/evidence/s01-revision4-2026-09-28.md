### First required gate that cannot pass yet

**Positive writer release remains unproved.** The approved route permits an
unknown escaped writer to hold the workspace and capacity indefinitely; the
fixture correctly did so, even after its observation deadline and after the
fixture processes exited. Its oracle knew exact PIDs, but those records were
deliberately unavailable to the candidate cleanup path. The installed App
Server's interrupt acknowledgement, `backgroundTerminals/clean` response and
empty list are not independent termination evidence: the preserved revision 3
trace recorded a delayed write about 10.6 ms after those signals. The generated
background-terminal list identified the registered command, not a complete,
race-safe set of escaped descendants. Releasing this old generation or claiming
exactly one safe replacement from those signals would contradict the contract.
No independently verified, production-applicable positive-release path was
demonstrated. This is the first critical release boundary reached by this
revision 4 validation that still lacks proof, so the
remaining admission, startup failure, lost-response/binding, overlapping
recovery, coordinator-crash, stale revision, full lifecycle, access and
transcript probes were not run or marked passed in this revision 4 run.

or a product scheduler. The hold, identity and isolated-login fixtures and
generated schema were removed after preserving the sanitized traces. The
observed writer, oracle and App Server PIDs were absent at final exact-PID
checks. No Stop, writer or capacity hold was released in the evidence.
